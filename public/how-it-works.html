The architecture behind
direct on-chain settlement.

Understand how ChainPay verifies EVM payments automatically without custody, third-party fees, or centralized gatekeepers.

1. The Principle: Direct vs Intermediaries

Traditional payment processors act as custodial middlemen. They take a percentage cut, hold your funds, and can freeze your payouts. ChainPay replaces the intermediary with pure blockchain verification.

Traditional Processors
  • ❌ 1.5% to 3% processing fees per transaction
  • ❌ Payout delays (T+2 to T+7 days)
  • ❌ Account freezing & chargeback risks
  • ❌ Mandatory third-party custody of your funds
ChainPay Model
  • ✅ 0% processor fees (you keep 100% of the funds)
  • ✅ Instant wallet-to-wallet transfer
  • ✅ Non-custodial: funds go directly to your address
  • ✅ Mathematical proof via Ethereum / EVM consensus

2. Step-by-Step Payment Lifecycle

Here is what happens from the second a customer initiates a payment to the moment your order is marked as fulfilled:

01

Customer Broadcasts Transaction

The buyer signs and broadcasts a transfer of the required ETH amount directly to your designated merchant wallet (using MetaMask, Coinbase Wallet, WalletConnect, or a script). The transaction enters the blockchain mempool.

02

Frontend Submits `txHash` to ChainPay

Once the transaction hash is received by the client application, it makes a lightweight HTTP POST request to: POST /api/tx with the txHash, expected amount, and your to merchant address.

03

Automated JSON-RPC Inspection

ChainPay queries an EVM JSON-RPC provider (Infura, Alchemy, or your own node) using Ethers.js:

  • Address Checksum: Compares recipient addresses using normalized EIP-55 casing to prevent phishing or typos.
  • BigInt Wei Precision: Verifies the exact transferred amount in 256-bit Wei, eliminating JavaScript floating-point errors.
  • Status Verification: Verifies that the transaction was not reverted on-chain.
04

Block Confirmation & Settlement

ChainPay waits for the block containing the transaction to be mined (waitForTransaction). Once confirmed, it records the settlement in the ledger and returns a 200 OK {"status": "confirmed"}, authorizing your backend to fulfill the order.

3. Security & Safety Guarantees

🛡️

Read-Only Server

Your verification server never needs your private key. It only requires a public read-only RPC URL to inspect transactions. Even if the server is compromised, your funds remain secure in your cold or hardware wallet.

🔒

Reorg & Double-Spend Protection

ChainPay awaits inclusion in a mined block before marking any order confirmed. Transactions that linger unconfirmed or get dropped by the mempool are never approved.

⚡

Multi-Network Ready

Works identically on Ethereum Mainnet, Sepolia, Arbitrum, Base, Polygon, Optimism, or BNB Chain by simply changing the RPC_URL in your configuration.
